# 5-Year Subscription Leak Calculator

Find out how much your subscriptions are really costing you.

The **5-Year Subscription Leak Calculator** is a free, interactive tool that helps you calculate the long-term cost of recurring subscriptions, understand where your money goes, and see what you could potentially buy instead.

Small monthly payments may not feel significant, but over five years, they can add up to a surprisingly large amount.

## Features

- **Multiple Subscriptions:** Add and manage multiple subscriptions.
- **Flexible Billing:** Support for monthly and annual billing.
- **Cancellation Delay Calculator:** Estimate how much extra you spend when you continue paying after wanting to cancel.
- **5-Year Cost Breakdown:** Calculate monthly, annual, and five-year subscription expenses.
- **Interactive Pie Chart:** Visualize how your subscription spending is distributed.
- **Multiple Currencies:** Supports USD, GBP, EUR, INR, CAD, and AUD.
- **Purchase Comparisons:** Discover illustrative alternatives you could afford with your subscription spending.
- **Responsive Design:** Clean interface designed for desktop and mobile screens.

## How It Works

1. Add your subscription name.
2. Enter its price.
3. Select monthly or annual billing.
4. Enter any additional months you continued paying after wanting to cancel.
5. Click calculate to see your results.

The calculator displays your estimated five-year spending, subscription breakdown, and purchase comparisons.

## Getting Started

No installation or complicated setup required.

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/anujsharma6y-ctrl/Subscription-Calculator.git
   ```

2. Open the project folder:

   ```bash
   cd Subscription-Calculator
   ```

3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

That's it!

## Currency Conversion

The calculator supports multiple currencies using approximate exchange rates.

**Note:** Exchange rates are illustrative and may not reflect current market rates. Update them periodically for more accurate conversions.

## Important Disclaimer

- Calculations assume subscription prices remain unchanged over the selected period.
- Purchase comparisons are illustrative, not live product prices.
- Actual subscription prices, taxes, and exchange rates may vary.
- This tool is intended for educational and budgeting purposes.
